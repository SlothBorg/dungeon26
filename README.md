# Dungeon 26

This work is released under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International.

This is a collection of rooms, encounters, and such for a MegaDungeon. One per day, each with a theme:

* **Entrances**
* **Liminal spaces** the spaces in between main events, hall ways, minor details etc.
* **Traps / Obstacles**
* **NPCs & Social Encounters**
* **Environmental Hazards** 
* **Treasures**
* **Rest Areas**
* **Lore**
* **Puzzles**
* and more as I think of them...

The goal is to stop worrying about a perfect template, system, etc and just write stuff for a dungeon. We shall see how well I hold to that.

---

After uncountable eons, the Blood War has ended.
The victors' malice unleashed upon the Prime Material.
The apocalypse echoed through the planes.
  Worlds burned,
  Gods fell,
  The Great Wheel shattered.

Upon a dead god cast adrift in the vastness of the Astral Sea
A small fortress was transformed by prophets who foresaw the doom, wise and mad wizards, and the ragged survivors into

Bastion, The Unbroken Gate, Journey's End, Final Haven, Last Plane, Remnant, Refuge, God's Reprieve, Dead Head's Rest

The last refuge has many names, but all know it as The City.
