# Dungeon 26

This is a collection of rooms, encounters, and such for a MegaDungeon. One per day, each with a theme:

* **Entrances**
* **Liminal spaces** the spaces in between main events, hall ways, minor details etc.
* **Traps / Obstacles**
* **NPCs & Social Encounters**
* **Environmental Hazards** 
* **Treasures**
* **Rest Areas**
* **Lore**
* **Puzzles**
* and more as I think of them...

The goal is to stop worrying about a perfect template, system, etc and just write stuff for a dungeon. We shall see how well I hold to that.

---

After eons uncounted, the Blood War ended.
The victors' malice was unleashed upon the Prime Material.
The apocalypse echoed through the planes.
  Worlds burned.
  Gods fell.
  The Great Wheel shattered.

Upon a dead god cast adrift in the Astral Sea
prophets who foresaw the doom, and ragged survivors
a refuge built from shattered remains of planes

Bastion, The Unbroken Gate, Journey's End, Final Haven, Last Plane, Remnant, Refuge, God's Reprieve, Dead Head's Rest

This final place has many names.
