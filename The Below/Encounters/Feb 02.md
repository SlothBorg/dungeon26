## Landmark

A pack of kobolds bickers loudly around a small fire, the smell of charred and burnt meat is in the air. 

You can't quite make out the words at this distance but one is gesturing wildly and another pushes a third to the ground, yelling something about burning and ruining the hunt.

## Hidden

The Kobolds are wary of outsiders, but starving, they recently killed a giant rat and were cooking it till one of them knocked it into the fire and it went up in a blaze of far and fur.

## Secret

One of the Kobolds is secretly a Gnome infiltrator in disguise and has a small bag of holding containing plenty of rations and drink.
