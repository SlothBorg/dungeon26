## Landmark

### The Well

An infamous inn / bar in The City, the central feature of it's large round main room is the titular well. A stone shaft descending hundreds of feet into the upper layers of the dungeons of The Below.

Customers may for a fee descend either by the stairs, or for extra coins in the comfort of one of the iron elevator cages.

At the bottom of the well shaft is a second bar, in which a large troll makes drinks and talks to adventures returning or steeling themselves for the delve. The area is kept secure by a hired force of mercenaries and a plethora of wards.

## Hidden

While the Troll doesn't like to talk about it, they were once an adventure and still keep several maps, notes, and other mementos from their days in The Below.

### Secret

The well allows a certain number of denizens of the Below up, for a fee of course, this is very much a crime and hidden from all prying eyes, the Troll see's it as it's duty now for unknown reasons.
