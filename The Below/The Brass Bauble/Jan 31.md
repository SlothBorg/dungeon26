## Landmark

The simple wooden door has a parchment sign tacked to the door with a dagger reads "Meeting in progress, do not disturb." 

As you open the door a book come hurling out at you, and an enraged Gnome yells "You Blind? or just dumb! Can't you read! Motherless stone fuckers!" the tirade of curses is stemmed as the door is slammed shut and you hear the unmistakable sound of it being bolted. 

After several long minutes, the door opens a crack and a kind orc woman slides out, "Apologize for my 'compatriot' there he's in quite a mood. Would you mind returning the book?"

## Hidden

The book contains a partial map and notes on a treasure located in a forgotten chapel among the catacombs of The Below.
The orc is willing to trade or buy the book back but hopes not to.
