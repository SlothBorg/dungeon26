## Landmark

### The fighting pits

A large round room with a sunken pit. The floor of the pit is sand covered, the walls are timbers chipped and cut from countless blows, splattered stains of gore decorate them.

Around the pit is an area for standing, and one side has amphitheater style benches rising above the pit, allowing for better viewing.

## Hidden



## Secret

Buried under the sands of this shabby arena is reliquary holding a malevolent spirit, this spirit will give blessing to those in the pits should it serve its dark plans. Those blessed find themselves driven into a blood lust often forgetting the manor of their victory and only returning to their senses after.
