### Sights & Structures

Light reflected off water, rickety wooden buildings on rafts of scavenged wood. Sheets of drying algae, forests of kelp swaying in the water.

Boats gliding across the water.

### Sounds

Hypnotic rhythmic water sounds.

### Smell and Taste

Brackish water, drying algae, fish, and rot.

### Lighting

Dim bioluminescent algae and lichen

### Common Monsters

Kuo-Toa, Large Fish, underwater plants.
