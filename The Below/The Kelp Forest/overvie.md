# The Kelp Forest

Deep underground lakes, the bottoms covered in thick forests of kelp. On the top raft villages of nomadic fish people the Kuo-toa

## Landmarks

### Kelp Forest

A single sentient forest of kelp, one entity that has grown intelligent. It has formed a symbiosis with the Kuo-Toa

### Raft City

Hundreds of Kuo-Toa raft homes, and buildings moored around a natural stone rising from the lake floor. Carved into the stone are stairs leading down to the kelp forest.

Large sheets of algae hand drying, when burned they produce noxious and blinding smoke. It also serves as their primary food source.

## LOCATION DETAILS

### Notables

**The Kuo-Toa of the kelp forest**, a singular hivemind, refer to themselves in the singular "I". They keep goods that they find to trade to adventures for services. The kelp the consume ritualistically to maintain the hivemind leaves them in a daze. They keep trinkets and treasures they find to trade with adventurers, otherwise they don't craft much that outsiders would value. They braid living kelp into the hair of adventurers to communicate with them, and kinda indoctrinate them.

**The Aboleth** A large fish tainted by strange planar energies, uses it's psionic powers to dominate beings that enter its caves. 

**Cult of the Jiggling Freedom** a breakaway sect of Kuo-Toa that has developed a resistance to the kelp, they are generally tolerated within the kelp forest as long as they don't make issues.
