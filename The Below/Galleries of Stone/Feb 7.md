## Public

The cave smells of acidic herbs and heated metal, the air is cool and dank.

In the center a large cauldron bubbles over red coals, scattered about at dull green bones they seem to be from some humanoid. 

A rack of alchemical supplies stands on the far wall.

## Hidden

The potion turns bones green.

## Secret

The Medusa's have been working on a way to slowly add poison to the potion they give to the giants to end the stalemate.
