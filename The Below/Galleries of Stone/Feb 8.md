## Public

### Kobold Jail

The natural stone gives way to a dug tunnel, ahead two large wooden doors clearly taken from some other structure have been set into a crude timber frame. "Kobol territory keep out!" has been scrawled in red paint. 

Tumbled rocks and dirt litter the area and you can see scuff marks to each side of the entry.

The doors open to a long hall with small barred rooms on each side, four per side at the far end of the hall a balcony provided a view over the area.

As you enter you hear a reptilian screetch that echos down the hall!

"HALT! Who enters!" 


## Hidden

The Kobolds while hostile are also curious and would like to avoid violence, they want to stay strong for taking over the caverns.
If violence breaks out, they will use the secrete passages to ambush the party.

## Secret

The Kobolds have been digging the secrete passages to expand their territory but also to create ambush spots like these though out.
