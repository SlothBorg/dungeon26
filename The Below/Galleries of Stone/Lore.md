## Factions

* **Coven of the Stone Snake** a group of medusas who worship a mythical entity known as the Stone Snake, or Stone Mother. They believe she lived in these caverns and have come here to conduct holy rites in her name. They were enraged to find the stone giants here and tried to violently expel them, upon failing they sued for peace and now pay them tribute in the form of potions.
* **Stone Giants** a tribe of Non-familial stone giants who have come to the depths of these caves to practice their arts and search for Stone Hearts, a rare gem that allows one to give life to stone. 
* **Stone Fang Tribe** a tribe of kobolds, who live in the small caves and cracks that are useless to both the meduas and giants. They trade with both, and know they both sides wish to see the other destroyed. They are all too willing to help both sides in the hopes they wipe each other out and they can seize the caves and all the spoils!
