## Public

The sloping path widens as it meander down into a large gallery full of stalactites and stalagmites, scattered amongst them are statues of various creatures. Most are damaged, but enough are depicted in terror and running away from something to cause you to pause. 

In the center a small still pool of crystal clear water glistens. 

Four passages lead out of this cave, one on the left wall, on far ahead and two on the right wall one half way across and the other at the end of the cavern.

## Hidden

Most of the "statues" are too old to be restored to life, but there is still a party here who could be rescued. 

## Secret

The pool itself is not magical but is used by the coven of medusas to scry on this room and get a sense of any who trespass.
