## Public

The cavern has a wall built across the far side, a door is set into the wall. The floor is cracked open a large jagged chasm runs most of the length of the room disappearing under the wall. 

The chasm descends deep into the dark, you can feel a cold chill rising from it, the sound of faint stone scrapping against stone.

To your left a small tunnel leads off into darkness.

## Hidden

The chasm descends nearly 100ft into a large cavern where Stone Giants tend a garden of living stone.
