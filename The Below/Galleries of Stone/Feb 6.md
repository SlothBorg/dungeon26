## Public

The natural cavern path, gives way to a hexagonal room neatly chiseled into the stone. Two large statues flank the entrance, you can feel their cold stone eyes upon you, in the center a raised dais into which three hooded and robed figures are pouring urns of some frothing liquid.

## Hidden

The three medusa's here are deep in ritual to maintain their alliance with the stone giants bellow, they are pouring down a potion to protect against their petrifying gaze. 

Should they fail it would be war with the giants.

## Secret

The giants secretly hope that the medusa's would renege on their deal so that they may force a better one.
