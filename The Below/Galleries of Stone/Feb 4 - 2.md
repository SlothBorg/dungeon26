## Public

The right hand path out of the main cavern leads into a cramped chamber, in which 19 statues have been moved. Judging by the deep gouges in the stone floor they were dragged here.

## Hidden

Each statue is a petrified person, most have died long ago and if reverted will crumble away. The three furthest from the entrance, an elf and two orcs are still alive.

## Secret

This is the queens personal collection, something she takes great pride in.
