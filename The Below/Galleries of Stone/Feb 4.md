## Public

The path opens onto a large cave, in front of you a wooden arch has been built from which hangs a thick musty curtain. Beyond raised dais holds a statue of a female medusa with shinning green eyes, in her left hand she holds the severed head of a man. At her feet a large bronze shield into which offerings have been tossed or burnt.

To the right stairs lead up to an alter flanked by two statues of men screaming in pain and terror while trying to run away from some unknown threat.

## Hidden

To the right of the alter, hidden behind a statue is a cleverly hidden secret tunnel, the door is a large stone which can be pushed away. A closer look indicates grooves on the stone floor and a slight draft.

The two statues flanking the alter, while appearing life like are in fact just masterfully carved statues.
