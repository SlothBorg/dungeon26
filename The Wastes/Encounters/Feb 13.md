## Public

A large jagged rock seems thrust up through the sand, large chains bind a tall muscular figure with black hair and eyes like embers to it.

The figure opens it's cracked mouth and only a crackling whisper escapes reminding you of the soft crackle of flames.

Gesturing only with their eyes it indicate a copper bucket of water at its feet. 

## Hidden

The Ifrit has been chained here by a wizard for many days, perhaps even years it has lost track of time. It will gladly strike deals with those who free it.

The key to its freedom lies in a wizards tower a days journey

## Secret

The Ifrit is good natured towards its rescuers taking on almost a paternal guardianship ... one that will more often than not cause trouble.
