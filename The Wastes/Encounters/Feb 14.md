## Public

A head of you the rocky ground wavers and ripples as if too hot, then the ground erupts rocks and dirt fly everywhere you catch glimpses of something large amongst it.

As the dirt and stones clear, before you a dull purple worm looms over you, its maw widening to show concentric rows of needle like teeth, the hot acidic breath hits you. 

## Hidden

The worm recently swallowed most of a caravan including part of a wagon, many of the goods might be recoverable.
