## Public

Four Gnomes strain against a long rope, struggling to pull a sailboat across the sands four more push the boat from behind. A single gnome sits in the crows nest with a spyglass they are facing away from you.

The gnomes make no secret of their intention to make it to the sea ad become great pirates.

## Hidden

The gnomes have, what they believe to be, a treasure map along with a collection of alchemist fire, grappling hooks, and cutlasses which they plan to use to make their fortune.
