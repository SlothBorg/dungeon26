## Public

A large pig barrels through the street! Oinking as it goes, knocking stuff out of its way. Behind it a group of robbed figured give chase panting and wheezing, while screaming "Stop her!", "Dai Ra! Please stop!", " transformed!" and similar. 

## Hidden

An experiment gone wrong has lead to the transformation into a pig. 

## Secret

Dai Ra, is a local alchemist and minor wizard. She's tried to stage an accident to help her get out of a bit of trouble related to an illicit affair but it has gone wrong.
