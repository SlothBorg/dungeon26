## Public

You crest a small rise and the grass fields roll out before you. Hundreds of large glass and stone structures dot the area before you, hooded figures in blue robes move between them, from this distances it is unclear what the devices are or what the figures are doing.

The devices are large conical flasks with a thing sheet of mirror forming a collar around the top. They are set atop stone pedestals with a metal tap set into the base allowing for the silvery liquid inside to be drained.

## Hidden

The devices are designed to collect and distill moon light.

## Secret

The arcane order who runs this, is secretly making deals with a coven of hags in exchange for dream stones. Which they use to steal researcher from a rival arcane order.
